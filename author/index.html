Seven Years Behind the Gate

A Story from Sainik School Korukonda

A memoir-inspired coming-of-age story based on seven formative years at Sainik School Korukonda — growing from strangers into close friends, and facing the emotional experience of leaving a place that became home.

Read the book →

The story

The story follows the journey of entering Sainik School at a young age and spending seven years behind its gates — growing from strangers into close friends and eventually facing the emotional experience of leaving.

FriendshipBrotherhoodHostel lifeDisciplineHomesicknessMischiefSchool traditionsGrowing up togetherIdentityDreams of serving the nation

For students, teenagers, former hostel students, people who experienced boarding school, and anyone who understands the nostalgia of growing up with friends in a shared place.

Memoir · Coming-of-age · Literary memoir-inspired fiction · Written by Seemalavalasa Naveen
