Based in India

I'm Seemalavalasa Naveen, a student, author and independent builder from India. I turn ideas into working products and explore the space where AI, software, cybersecurity, autonomous agents, civic technology and creative technology meet.

The Journey

My work ranges from AI-powered applications and autonomous systems to cybersecurity tools, experimental simulations and real-world problem-solving projects.

Alongside technology I am also an author. My first book, Seven Years Behind the Gate, is a memoir-inspired story based on seven formative years of boarding-school life at Sainik School Korukonda.

My approach is simple: Learn → Build → Experiment → Ship → Learn again.
